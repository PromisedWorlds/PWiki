# Kevba's Anomaly

A wormhole that connects the Kerbolar system with the Debdeb system. Orbits Gurdamma. Its other side is located in Dres orbit.

To use wormholes, they must be enabled in Promised Worlds' settings, and [Singularity](https://github.com/LGhassen/Singularity) must be installed.
